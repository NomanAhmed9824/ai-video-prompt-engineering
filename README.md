## Step-by-Step: How I Create Character Sheet (Identity Lock)

### Step 1: Reference Images
- Front face
- Side angle (optional)
- Full body / outfit

### Step 2: Identity Lock

```text
CHARACTER LOCK:
@character1

Inherit exactly:
- Face shape and features
- Skin tone and natural texture
- Hair style, color and length
- Body proportions
- Exact outfit and accessories

Do NOT inherit:
- Background
- Original pose
- Original lighting
- Camera angle from reference
```

### Step 3: Strong Example

```text
CHARACTER LOCK: Young South Asian woman, 24 years old, natural skin with light texture, long straight black hair, soft facial features, wearing oversized beige hoodie and simple gold hoop earrings. Keep exact face and body proportions across all shots.
```
